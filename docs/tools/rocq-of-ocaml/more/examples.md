The main project handled with `rocq-of-ocaml` is the crypto-currency [Tezos](https://tezos.com/). The result is in the project:
* `coq-tezos-of-ocaml`: https://formal-land.gitlab.io/coq-tezos-of-ocaml/

providing a translation in Rocq of the economic protocol of Tezos.

We are now focusing on:
* maintaining the translation of the protocol of Tezos, as the protocol evolves;
* improving this translation by removing some (supposedly safe) axioms;
* applying `rocq-of-ocaml` to other OCaml programs.
